SQL (using Postgres full-text search with websearch_to_tsquery):
SELECT product_id, product_name, product_type, segment, rate_apy,
       ts_rank(search_vector, websearch_to_tsquery('english', 'wealth advisory affluent')) AS relevance_score
FROM meridian_bank.products
WHERE search_vector @@ websearch_to_tsquery('english', 'wealth advisory affluent')
ORDER BY relevance_score DESC;
